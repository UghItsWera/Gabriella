# 🎧 Gabriella — Psychological Horror VR Experience

## 👻 Overview
**Gabriella** is a psychological horror VR game developed in Unity for the Oculus Quest platform. The player is guided through a dark, immersive environment by the ghost of Gabriella — a central narrative character fully voiced and performed by me.

The experience focuses on atmosphere, tension, and narrative-driven horror, blending environmental storytelling with VR immersion to create a deeply unsettling psychological experience.

Link to the download page: https://weramichalek.itch.io/gabriella 

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Concept
Players follow Gabriella, a ghostly presence who acts as both guide and narrative anchor throughout the experience. As the story unfolds, the player is drawn deeper into a distorted environment where reality becomes increasingly unstable.

The game focuses on:
- 🧠 Psychological horror and narrative tension  
- 👁️ Immersive VR exploration  
- 🎧 Voice-led storytelling (Gabriella is fully voiced by me)  
- 🌫️ Atmospheric environmental design

## 🧪 Installation & Play Instructions

This game is distributed as a standalone VR build for Oculus Quest devices.

### 📲 How to Play:
1. Download the APK build (available in releases / later itch.io page)  
2. Install **SideQuest** on your PC  
3. Connect your Oculus Quest headset  
4. Sideload the APK using SideQuest  
5. Launch the game from your headset library
